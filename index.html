01 — About

I'm a data scientist, and I like problems. Give me a messy question and a pile of data, and I want to know what's really going on and why.

What I enjoy most is the story behind the data: how people behave, how technology changes things, and how we each see the world. This site is where I chase those stories and try to show them in more interesting ways than a chart in a slide deck, as interactive stories and games.
