01 — About

I work where analysis meets strategy. My job is to turn a vague business question, like "why is growth slowing?" or "where should we invest next?", into a clear answer and a recommendation that leaders can act on.

Before analytics, I [your background, e.g. studied economics, worked in consulting or ran operations]. That taught me to ask "so what?" before "how?". I'm hands-on with the data, but I measure my work by the decisions it changes, not the queries it takes. Outside work I write long-form data stories that explain the economy and the world through numbers.

Start with the decision

Before touching data, I pin down what decision the work will inform and what would change someone's mind.

Find the "so what"

Numbers aren't insight. I dig until I can say what it means for the business and what to do next.

Tell it as a story

One clear narrative, built around the chart that matters, rather than forty slides of caveats.
