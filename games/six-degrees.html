A Premier League game

Six degrees of James Milner

Every one of the 5,174 players in our Premier League data can reach any other through a chain of club teammates. No chain needs more than five links, and more than eight in ten pairs need three or fewer.

Data: Transfermarkt, Fantasy Premier League and Wikidata · Updated November 2026

      Try

      Get from the first player to the second by naming teammates. Each player you name must have played for the same club in the same season as the last one. Like Pointless, the lower your score the better: obscure players, short overlaps and clubs that rarely made the Premier League score fewest points.

        How it works

        Two players are linked if they both played for the same club in the same Premier League season. The data covers every player who appeared in the league from 1992–93 to gameweek 6 of 2026–27. James Milner sits at the centre: nobody is more than three links from him.

        In the game, every player you name scores points, as in Pointless, and lower is better. Famous players, long overlaps and clubs that are always in the league score most. Little-known players, single shared seasons and clubs with only a season or two in the Premier League score least.

        Data: Transfermarkt appearances (to 2024–25), the Fantasy Premier League archive (2025–26 onwards) and Wikidata (gaps before 2009). A few pre-2009 squad players who never played may be included.
